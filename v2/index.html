Getting There

Travel & Stay

By Train

Jhansi Junction is the nearest major station, about 18 km (around 30 minutes by road) from Orchha, with express trains from Delhi and across India.

By Air

Gwalior Airport is the nearest, about 120 km (around 2½ hours by road). Khajuraho Airport is about 170 km (around 3 hours).

Your Stay

All guests will be staying with us at Raj Vilas The Palace.
